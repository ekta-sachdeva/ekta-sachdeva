### 📊 Featured Project

#### [Retail Distribution Portfolio — Account Health & Net Retention Analysis](https://github.com/ekta-sachdeva/retail-sfa-portfolio-health)

An independent case study modeling a **$10.1M ARR portfolio** across 50 simulated retail distributor accounts.

- 📈 Designed a weighted **0–100 account health scoring model** (40% field rep adoption, 25% order volume trend, 20% support burden, 15% executive sponsorship) with Green/Yellow/Red risk tiering
- 💰 Modeled the full retention waterfall to calculate **97.1% NRR vs. 88.5% GRR**, surfacing an 8.6-point spread where expansion revenue was masking underlying churn risk
- 📝 Authored a 45-day, Gainsight-style intervention playbook for at-risk accounts, including a stakeholder outreach email template
- 📂 Fully documented methodology, formulas, and model limitations, published as a structured GitHub repository

**[→ View the repository](https://github.com/ekta-sachdeva/retail-sfa-portfolio-health)**
